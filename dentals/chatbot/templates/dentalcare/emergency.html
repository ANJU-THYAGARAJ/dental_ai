EMERGENCY

Get urgent professional help when needed

A chatbot cannot safely assess a serious emergency. If you have a life-threatening situation, contact your local emergency service immediately.

Warning signs

  • Difficulty breathing or swallowing
  • Rapidly increasing facial, mouth or neck swelling
  • Uncontrolled heavy bleeding
  • Major facial or jaw trauma
  • Loss of consciousness or severe systemic symptoms

AI emergency screening

Describe what is happening and the chatbot will look for red flags. This is only a screening aid.
